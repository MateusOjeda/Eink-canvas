# Privacy Policy — EinkCanvas

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

EinkCanvas is an application designed to help users manage and display images on compatible electronic ink (e-ink) devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how EinkCanvas handles information when you use the application and its related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, EinkCanvas may process the following information:

- **Account information:** Authentication identifiers used to provide access to the application's services.
- **Device information:** Device identifiers and configuration data needed to associate an e-ink device with your account.
- **Images and collections:** Images you upload or select, along with related metadata, such as collection identifiers and display preferences.
- **Synchronization data:** Information needed to synchronize images, device settings, and display status between the application and your e-ink device.
- **Technical information:** Information processed by the services used to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the application.

EinkCanvas does not intentionally request sensitive personal information as a requirement for its core functionality.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by EinkCanvas is used to:

- Provide the application's core features.
- Store and organize images and collections.
- Synchronize images and settings with compatible e-ink devices.
- Authenticate users and associate devices with their configurations.
- Maintain the reliability and security of the application.

## 4. Third-Party Services

EinkCanvas uses Firebase services provided by Google to support certain application features, which may include authentication, database storage, and image storage.

These services process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

EinkCanvas may also integrate with third-party services when users choose to use features that depend on those services. Any such integration is subject to the applicable service's terms and privacy policy.

## 5. Data Sharing

EinkCanvas does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the application, provide requested functionality, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality and fulfill legitimate operational requirements.

Users who wish to delete their information or request assistance with data deletion may contact the developer using the contact information provided below.

Some information may remain in backups or be retained when required by law.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

EinkCanvas is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13, and the application does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13 as a specific feature.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact the developer.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application evolves. The latest version will be made available at the same public URL.

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the EinkCanvas project repository:
